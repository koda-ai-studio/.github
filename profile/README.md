We build and implement open-source, self-hosted software, backed by hands-on consulting and tailored engineering. We help organizations manage security risk and compliance evidence, connect systems, and turn data into useful analytics. Through data pipelines, automation, and agentic workflows, we reduce manual work and give teams time back.

## Free, open-source software

Run our tools in your own environment, with your infrastructure, identity, and data under your control.

| Project and quickstart | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [agent-bom](https://github.com/koda-ai-studio/agent-bom#readme) | Security scanning and a self-hosted control plane for AI, MCP, and cloud infrastructure. |
| [grc-lake](https://github.com/koda-ai-studio/grc-lake#readme) | Collect read-only compliance evidence, evaluate controls, and export reviewable audit workpapers. |

Both projects are available under the Apache-2.0 license and are free to use and self-host. You cover your own infrastructure and any third-party service costs. Consulting services are optional.
